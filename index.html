IOK Access Courses

IOK Access offers foundational courses in Classical Arabic, Fiqh, Hadith, Qur’an, and Spiritual Refinement—designed to help students build a strong base in Islamic learning. With once-a-week classes and flexible onsite, online, and on-demand options, IOK Access makes higher Islamic education accessible for busy learners seeking steady, meaningful growth. IOK Takhassus Courses

The Graduate Certificate in Islamic Thought and Scholarship is a year-long hybrid program combining classical ijazah-based learning with modern academic study. Guided by Dr. Omar Qureshi, students explore the Qur’an, Hadith, Law, Theology, and Islam’s engagement with modernity—building the depth and perspective needed for advanced scholarship and community leadership.
